# MyceliumCraft

Play Mycelium as a Minecraft player:
- A Minecraft Fabric mod and a Mycelium mod talk through shared memory.
- Minecraft controls the player, and Mycelium shows the world.


## Notes
- I only tested this with keyboard and mouse. I have no idea what'll happen with a controller
- I only tested this on Windows. I have no idea what'll happen with other OSs
- Once loaded into a profile, you can access the Minecaft pause menu with "Esc" and the Mycelium one through 'P'
- I reccomend using GUI scale 3 (GUI scale 4 for screens larger than 1920x1080) in Minecraft's video settings. It probably looks fine on the other numbers but I didn't test them.
- You can use the Minecraft pause menu to change the Mycelium hotkeys (scroll all the way to the bottom to find the Mycelium section).
- Be default, you can press 'B' to access the emote menu. If you do an emote, other spores may copy you.
  - The hotkey is changeable. It's under the EmoteCraft section in the hotkeys menu.
- F5 to change camera views, just like normal Minecraft.
- Remember that in the Mycelium pause menu there is a render setting (I forgot about this for a bit and o boy is the Delta Crag laggy).
- I may look into Multiplayer one day idk


## Requirements

- Mycelium on Steam
- Minecraft Java 26.3 with Fabric Loader 0.19.5 (I recommend PrismLauncher)


## Installation

- Download
  - Download the MyceliumCraft.zip file from the Releases page
  - Extract the zip and keep the folders inside handy. They'll be your secret mousekatools for later.
- Mycelium Side
  - Install Mycelium on Steam
  - Right click it and hover over "Manage"
  - Click on "Browse local files" (This take you to Steam's Mycelium folder)
  - Take the all the contents of the "MyceliumCraft MYCELIUM" folder and drag them into Steam's Mycelium folder
- Mycelium Side UNINSTALLATION
  - Right click Mycelium and hover over "Manage"
  - Click on "Browse local files" (This take you to Steam's Mycelium folder)
  - Just delete everything in Steam's Mycelium folder
  - Back in Steam, right click on Mycelium
  - Click on "Properties"
  - Click on "Installed Files"
  - Click on "Verify integrity of game files"
- Minecraft Side
  -  Have Minecraft 26.3 with Fabric Loader 0.19.5
  - Find your launcher's equivalent of the ".minecraft" folder (google if you don't know). Inside of it should be 2 folders named "mods" and "config". If they aren't there yet, then you can create them
  - Find the "mods" and "config" folders inside the "MyceliumCraft MINECRAFT" folder
  - Drag the contents of those folders into your own Minecraft's respective folders


## Playing

- Launch Minecraft FIRST
- If playing for the first time, create a new singleplayer world
  - Cycle through the game modes until you find the "Mycelium" game mode
  - Create the world. You shouldn't have to mess with any other settings
- If returning to MyceliumCraft
  - Load into your previously created singleplayer world
- Alt+Tab out of the game (preferably without pausing the game)
- Launch Mycelium
- Create/Load into any profile
